Logging payments for you

Tapping your real card on a shop’s machine can’t pay through Funcial: only the bank or Google Wallet can do that. What Funcial can do is notice the payment right after and log it for you. There are four ways, and they work together. Each one is off until you turn it on.

What I need from you before building this
  • A screenshot of the notification your bank (and Google Wallet, if you use it) shows after you pay. Each bank words it differently, and I read the shop and amount out of that text.
  • If your bank texts you after payments, a screenshot of one of those texts.
  • A statement file from your bank’s website (CSV if it offers one, otherwise PDF). Blur or change the numbers if you like; I only need the layout.

Good to know: reading texts needs Android’s SMS permission. Google Play only allows that for messaging apps, so it works for the version you install from the download page, not a Play Store version. Notifications don’t have that problem. My recommendation: start with notifications, the home screen button and statement import, and add texts only if your bank doesn’t send app notifications.
