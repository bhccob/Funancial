Switching currency, for real

Today “Change currency” only renames the money: Afl. 1,200 turns into US$ 1,200, which is wrong. The fix: your money stays kept in florins, and a switch shows the whole app in florins, dollars or euros, converted. The florin is pegged at 1.79 to the dollar, so dollars never need the internet. Euros use today’s rate, which the app already fetches for Invest.
