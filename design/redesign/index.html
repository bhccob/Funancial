Where to look

The dark hero card is always first, and it shows how the month is going in one bar. Every section after it is its own soft card with a title, so the page reads top to bottom.

Buttons you can find

Actions are 58px tiles with a word under each icon. Nothing is icon-only, and every tap target is at least 48px for Android.

Pictures, not just numbers

A month bar, a category donut, daily bars, limit rings, a net-worth trend, goal rings and calendar tiles for bills. Every chart has a legend and shows exact numbers when you tap it.

Navigation

Five slots: Home, Budget, a big + to add, Quests and Menu. The floating + button goes away, and Invest and Buy it? live in the Menu until they're built.
