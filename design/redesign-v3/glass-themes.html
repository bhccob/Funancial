Glass, in black and white, with themes in the Shop

Soft glowing shapes sit behind everything, and the glass on top blurs them: the buttons, the cards, the bill bubbles, the Overview tiles and the chart. Your Available balance sits straight on the top block with no box around it, the lists are plain rows with thin lines between them, and the bottom bar is a normal tab bar. Black & White is free. Colors are themes you buy in the Shop.
