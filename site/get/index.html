What's new

Safe and simple

Your money lives on your phone, encrypted. An account keeps a locked copy so you can move phones. No ads.

Not financial advice.

Why Android asks

Android asks before installing apps that don't come from the Play Store, and that's normal for an app shared between friends.

What the app asks for

  • The internet, to look up prices, to keep your locked copy if you have an account, and to check for a new version.
  • Notifications, to show the reminders you set. It may wake the phone for a moment to show one.
  • Start after a restart, to bring your reminders back.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock, if you turn it on. Older phones list this as a fingerprint permission.

How to install

  1. Tap Download, then open the file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install, open Funancial, and try demo money first.
