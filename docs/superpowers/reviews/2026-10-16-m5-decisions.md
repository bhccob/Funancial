## The calls I made, in plain words

- **Dividends.**
  - Expected payouts come from Yahoo's history (real ex-dates) plus its announced next payout, then step forward by the inferred frequency.
  - An unknown pay date is assumed to be 14 days after the ex-date, and guessed dates show "~".
  - A confirmed payout settles any payout within half a step of it, so a confirmed dividend never comes back.
  - Dividends earned before a full sale can still be confirmed, but not reinvested.
- **Prices and currency.**
  - Prices and exchange rates are today's, with no history. So the net-worth trend and the holding chart's older points use today's rate. A sold position's value before the sale is the sale amount.
  - The Invest currency is display-only. Stored money stays in your app currency, and totals are built from converted rows, so they add up.
  - The Settings currency switch relabels and doesn't convert. It only offers currencies with the same decimals.
- **Selling.**
  - The price per share is typed in your app currency.
  - A partial sale takes the average cost.
  - A full sale removes the holding but keeps a snapshot, so it can be undone from History.
  - Typing the share count as shown sells everything.
- **Profit & loss** = gain on what you hold + realised profit from sales + every confirmed dividend.
- **Kept as built:**
  - The 12-month dividend bars are narrower than 44px.
  - Two-slice pies stay pies, because you asked for a pie per type.
  - Refresh sits on its own row.
  - A confirmed dividend counts as logged that day.
